About

Vivek Reddy

I build practical machine-learning systems — LLM pipelines, retrieval-augmented applications, and OCR and document-understanding tooling for messy, real-world input.

Alongside that, I’ve built a multi-agent job-search system, an enterprise RAG chatbot, and LLM-driven knowledge-graph construction — see Projects.

I hold a B.Tech in Mathematics and Computing from IIT Guwahati.

I’m currently looking for internship and full-time opportunities in AI/ML engineering, applied research, and backend systems.
