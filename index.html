About

I work at the practical end of machine learning. The interesting part is rarely the model — it is everything around it: getting scanned engineering drawings, PDFs, and live audio into a clean, structured form, and keeping the output grounded enough to trust.

Currently, I am an AI/ML intern at Flagestrator, where I build PaddleOCR pipelines that pull equipment tags, pipelines, and annotations out of P&ID engineering diagrams into structured JSON, and low-latency live transcription on top of the Sarvam LiveKit SDK.

I hold a B.Tech in Mathematics and Computer Science from IIT Guwahati. My own projects keep circling the same problem: retrieval that stays honest — LangChain and LangGraph orchestration, ChromaDB and FAISS vector search, Neo4j knowledge graphs, and FastAPI services behind JWT, containerised and shipped through CI.

Right now, I am drawn to retrieval-augmented systems, multi-agent orchestration, document and diagram understanding, model deployment, and the unglamorous work of making these pipelines reproducible instead of impressive once.
